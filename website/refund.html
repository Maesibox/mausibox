Refund Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

30-day money-back guarantee. If you are not happy with Nugget Compass, you can request a full refund within 30 days of your purchase – for a new subscription and for a renewal. No questions asked.

How to request a refund

  • Use the link in your purchase receipt e-mail from Paddle, or go to paddle.net and look up your order, or
  • e-mail us at lehmann.marcel@icloud.com with the e-mail address you used for the purchase.

Refunds are processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record, to the original payment method. It may take a few business days until the money is back on your account.

After a refund

Your paid account or club subscription ends when the refund is issued. Your points, tracks and claims stay on your device and can still be exported.

Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time. It then stays active until the end of the paid period and is not renewed. After the 30-day refund window, we do not refund partial periods unless required by law.

Right of withdrawal (EU/UK consumers)

If you are a consumer in the EU or UK, you also have a statutory right of withdrawal of 14 days. Our 30-day money-back guarantee covers and extends this right.
