Where to find gold – and where you're allowed to.

Nugget Compass is a map app for recreational gold prospectors. Rules and permits per country, gold places, experiences and clubs worldwide – in one app that also works offline in the field.

Open the app See pricing

Everything for your next gold trip

Built by a prospector with club claims in Arizona and gold panning roots in Switzerland.

⚖️

Rules per country

Is hobby panning allowed? Which permit do you need, and where do you get it? Tap a country – 40 countries, plus rules per US state, Australian state, Canadian province and Swiss canton.

🧭

Travel cheat sheet

One page per country: rules, permits, experiences, gold places and clubs – ready before you book your flight.

🎟

Experiences

Official panning sites, show mines, guided tours, courses and detector hire around the world.

🗺

Claims & land status

Active and closed BLM mining claims, land ownership and protected areas in the US; mining titles in Canada, Australia and New Zealand.

🪨

Geology layers

Geological maps, faults, aeromagnetics, LiDAR relief and known gold occurrences from official geological surveys.

📍

Your own points & tracks

Save finds with photos, record detector tracks at coil width, mark your parking spot, export to GPX/KML – also offline.

For prospecting clubs

Give your members one map with your club's claims – and get new guests from travellers who use the app.

🔑

Invite codes & admins

Members join with an invite code or QR code. Club admins manage members and roles.

▣

Shared club claims

Upload your claim files or record claims with location monument and corner posts. Only your members see them.

🏕

Guest page

Show visitors what your club offers – guest passes, camping, outings – and answer guest requests right in the app.

💬

Club chat & BLM alerts

Talk to your members and get notified about new and closed BLM claims around your area.
