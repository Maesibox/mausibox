3. The service

The App is a web map application for recreational gold prospectors and prospecting clubs. It combines publicly available maps and data from official sources with tools for your own points, tracks, claims and clubs.

  • Free: the map with public layers, rules per country, gold places, experiences and the travel cheat sheet.
  • Prospector account (USD 20 / CHF 20 / EUR 20 per year): login, sync of points, tracks and photos across devices, BLM claim change alerts, guest requests to clubs, joining clubs with an invite code.
  • Club subscription (USD 1 per member and year, billed yearly per club): a club with invite codes and admins, shared club claims and claim files, club chat, a guest page and guest requests.
