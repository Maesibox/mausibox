Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains which personal data Nugget Compass processes. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for users in the EU/EEA and UK, the GDPR.

1. Controller

Marcel Lehmann, Klosenmatt 9a, 6043 Adligenswil, Switzerland, lehmann.marcel@icloud.com.

2. Using the App without an account

Without an account your data stays on your device. Your points, photos, tracks, claims, parking spot and settings are stored only in your browser's storage. We do not receive them. Your location (GPS) is used only on your device when you turn on the location function, and is stored only when you save a point, parking spot or track yourself.

3. Using the App with an account

With an account we store:

  • user name, e-mail address and password (encrypted only),
  • your club memberships and roles, and invite codes used,
  • login attempts (user name and time) to prevent abuse,
  • claims you share with a club, and files uploaded by club admins,
  • club chat messages (visible to the members of that club),
  • club guest pages and guest requests (visible to the club admins and the requesting user),
  • if you use device sync: your points, tracks and photos (private, only visible to you).

We need this data to provide your account, the club features and sync (performance of contract).

4. Payment

Payments are processed by Paddle.com as Merchant of Record. You enter your payment details directly with Paddle; we do not see them. We receive whether and for what you paid, and your name, e-mail address and country for billing. Paddle's privacy policy also applies.

5. Service providers and data location

  • Supabase Inc. (database, login, photo storage) – servers in the USA (West US, Oregon).
  • Hosting of the App and website: GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA) and Hoststar (Switzerland). Server logs contain technically necessary data such as IP address and time of access.
  • Paddle.com, see section 4.

Some providers are located outside Switzerland and the EU (e.g. in the USA). We transfer data there only with appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses) or where an adequate level of protection exists.

6. Map services

The App loads maps, aerial images and data directly from the servers of the respective providers (authorities and data services in Switzerland, the EU, the USA, Canada, Australia and New Zealand – see "About & data sources" in the App menu). These providers technically receive your IP address and the map area shown.

7. No advertising, no tracking

The App and this website use no advertising or analytics cookies and no tracking. The App stores in your browser only what it needs to work (settings, offline maps, login).

8. Retention

We keep account data as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days. Chat messages and claims shared with a club may remain visible to club members until a club admin deletes them. Accounting records are kept as long as the law requires (10 years in Switzerland).

9. Your rights

You can request access to your data, have incorrect data corrected and have data deleted, and you can ask for a copy of your data in a common format. Write to lehmann.marcel@icloud.com. In Switzerland you can complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU/UK to the data protection authority of your country.

10. Changes

We update this policy when the App changes. The version published here applies.
